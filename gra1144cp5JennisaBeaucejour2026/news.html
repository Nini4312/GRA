    Olivia Dean is having a breakout 2026, recently winning three major awards—Album of the Year (The Art of Loving), Song of the Year ("Man I Need"), and Best Female Act—at the 30th Anniversary MOBO Awards in Manchester. This success follows her four-win sweep at the Brit Awards, cementing her status as a premier UK artist with charting hits like "So Easy (To Fall in Love)"
